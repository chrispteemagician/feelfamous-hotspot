CannaTV.nl

An international 420 platform from the Netherlands. At Spannabis 2026, they pointed a camera at a bloke from Blackburn with a suitcase full of software and said: "Of course we said YES."

“At Spannabis we met this amazing gentleman @spicylister from the UK with a very special message and he asked if he could share it with you all… Of course we said YES because Canna TV is the ultimate 420 platform for stoners all around the world.”

CannaTV.nl don't have a website. They don't need one. They show up, point a camera at the people worth filming, and share it with the world. That is the job. They did it for Doc at Spannabis Bilbao 2026 without being asked, without a contract, without a pitch deck.

The reel they posted linked to cannabin-oid.co.uk and feelfamous.com and reached the global cannabis community. That reel is now the opening line of every outreach email Chris sends to Spannabis contacts. Every door it opened started with CannaTV saying yes.

This page exists because credit matters. They gave a voice to someone who had been trying to find one for a long time. That deserves a page.
