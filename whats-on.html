Everything below is already on your phone. No app, no sign-in, no Google account, no waiting for signal in the woods. Just the whole weekend, in one place, free to keep and free to pass on.

Who made this & why This isn’t an official Wild Gardens or Tunes Festivals page — it’s put together by Chris, parked at the Chill Zone this weekend, because the official info is scattered across Instagram posts and a gated third-party app that a lot of people are finding frustrating — wants your Google account, barely works without signal, and the reviews say as much. Quick facts

📍 Where
Port Eliot Estate, St Germans, Cornwall, PL12 5PD (off the B3249)
🚗 Car park & campsite open
Thursday from 12pm (Instagram has said 9am too — get there whenever suits, worst case you wait a bit)
🏕️ Campsite closes
Monday 12pm
🎪 Arena hours
Gates 12pm (some listings say 11am) — closes 11pm nightly. After-parties in the woods run to 1am.
🎟️ Thursday
Arena entry on Thursday is a paid add-on — not included in a standard weekend ticket.
↩️ Re-entry
Allowed until 7pm. None after that. Under-18s can’t re-enter at all.
🍺 What you can bring into camp
12 cans, or 2 bottles of wine decanted into plastic (not glass), or a mix — per person, one go on first entry. No spirits. Food/drink is campsite-only, not allowed in the arena.
🚫 Not allowed
No dogs anywhere on site, including camping. Glass looks to be a no as well — not spelled out for the whole site, but assume it and you won’t get caught out.
♿ Accessibility
Free carer ticket for registered carers (bring proof + ID to box office). Accessible parking ~200m from the entrance, wheelchair viewing area, disabled toilets. Ask a steward on arrival.

Stages & areas

  • Main Stage — set in the formal gardens, the big headline sets
  • Quarry Stage — a natural amphitheatre carved into the estate, smaller and more intimate
  • The Forest / Woodland — deep bass, after-parties running to 1am
  • Silent Disco — headphones, deep in the woods
  • Wellbeing Sanctuary — not music, see below, mornings 9am–12pm

Wellbeing & activities

Sessions run 9am–12pm daily at the Wellbeing Sanctuary: yoga (dynamic, silent with headsets, restorative/Reiki), sound baths, guided meditation, ice bath + breathwork, sauna, hot tub, singing workshop, a guided run round the grounds, women’s and men’s wellbeing circles, aromatherapy, sleep and digital-detox talks.

Elsewhere on site: paddleboarding on the river (book ahead: supnewquay@gmail.com, or ask on site), canoeing, archery, family fun runs, and Bubbly & Brush painting sessions. There’s river/estuary wild swimming too — ask a steward where’s safe on the day.
