The Chill Zone

You don’t have to
perform right now.

See the van? That’s Chris — Chris P Tee, also known as Doc. He’s parked up, shade’s out, quiet liquid drum and bass is playing. This spot exists for one reason: somewhere to actually breathe.

If the festival’s a lot right now — too loud, too bright, too many people, too much of everything — come and sit down. No entry fee. No small talk required. No performance required, from you or anyone.

Magic happens when the moment calls for it, not on a schedule. Kids get puppets and characters and made to feel genuinely magical. Adults get somewhere to stop. It’s an AuDHD-aware space — positive vibes only, nobody’s judged for being quiet, loud, weird, or somewhere in between.

Talk about the weird stuff

Chris likes talking about the things most small talk avoids — AuDHD, mental health, radio, old tech, whatever's actually on your mind. If you want a proper conversation instead of festival chit-chat, that's exactly what this spot is for.

If you spot an aerial sticking out of the van, that's not decoration — there's CB, ham radio, and a MeshCore off-grid comms node running alongside the WiFi hotspot. If any of that means something to you, come and say hello. If it doesn't, ignore it completely; it changes nothing about the shade and the quiet.

Never used a CB radio? Curious what one even is? Ask. Chris will pull one out and show you how it works, on the spot — same thing he was doing with them as a kid.
